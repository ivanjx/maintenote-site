Common questions

Do I need an account or internet connection?

No account is required. Adding things, recording maintenance, updating usage readings, and reviewing history work offline. App downloads and software updates need an internet connection.

Why am I not getting reminders?

In MainteNote, open Settings and check that Notifications is on. In your iPhone's Settings, allow notifications for MainteNote and check whether Focus or Scheduled Summary is delaying them. Also check that the maintenance schedule is enabled and its due date or usage reading is correct.

Open MainteNote periodically so it can refresh upcoming reminders. iOS limits how many notifications an app can schedule. Usage-based reminders depend on readings you enter; MainteNote does not automatically measure mileage or operating hours.

Why does MainteNote ask for camera or photo access?

These permissions let you attach photos to things and service records. Photos are optional. You can change permissions in your iPhone's Settings and keep using maintenance tracking without adding photos.

Where are my records? Can you recover them?

Records and attached photos are stored in the app on your iPhone, not in a MainteNote account. We cannot view or restore your local records. MainteNote does not provide its own cloud backup or sync service. Device backups are managed separately by Apple and your settings.

Do not delete the app or reset its data as a troubleshooting step if you need to keep your records. Contact support first.

How do I delete my data?

You can delete individual things and history entries in the app. To clear MainteNote's local records and app-managed photos, open Settings and choose Reset all data. This action cannot be undone. Copies in your Photos library, device backups, or emails are managed separately.

Are the suggested maintenance intervals right for my equipment?

Presets are starting points. Adjust each interval to match the manufacturer's instructions, your equipment, and how you use it.
